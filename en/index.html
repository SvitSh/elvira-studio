About me

Beauty is not about trying to look younger

To me, beauty means well-cared-for skin, a lively expression and feeling at home with your reflection. I am Elvira, and I have worked in beauty and wellbeing for around ten years.

What matters most to me is not only the treatment, but the person. I want every client to have a calm, careful and personal moment.

My path to becoming a cosmetologist began at 53, when my nearly 30-year career in finance ended and I had to rebuild my life. In 2022, war brought me to Finland. Now, at 65, I am starting my own business in Kokkola.
